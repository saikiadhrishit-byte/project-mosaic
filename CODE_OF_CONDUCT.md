# Contributor Code of Conduct

Project Mosaic is an open research and architecture repository. We value respectful technical discussion, constructive criticism, careful research, and honest documentation.

## Community Expectations

We expect contributors to:

- communicate clearly and professionally;
- give thoughtful feedback grounded in technical reasoning;
- avoid personal attacks or dismissive behavior;
- make room for people with different engineering backgrounds;
- distinguish hypotheses from implementation claims.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- harassment or discriminatory language;
- personal attacks or demeaning comments;
- dismissing or mocking technical criticism;
- gatekeeping through intimidation or status-based pressure;
- repeated disruption of constructive discussion.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, please describe the issue clearly and respectfully through a private or project-maintained channel. The repository maintainers will review reports and take action as needed.

This project is intentionally small and research-oriented. It should remain welcoming to people who care about engine architecture, compiler design, systems engineering, and open-source collaboration.
